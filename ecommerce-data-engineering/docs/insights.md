## 8. Prioritized Business Recommendations

1. **Logistics SLA Intervention**: Focus carrier renegotiation or regional fulfillment centers on states with `pct_late > 15%` to lift marketplace NPS/reviews.
2. **Checkout Estimate Calibration**: Improve delivery-date estimation buffer on long-haul routes to prevent severe `8+ Days Late` review penalties.
3. **Retention & Repeat-Buyer Program**: Convert `Potential Loyalist` customers into repeat buyers within 60 days of their first delivered order.
4. **Catalog Hygiene**: Enforce mandatory category tagging at seller onboarding to eliminate `"unknown"` SKU listings.
